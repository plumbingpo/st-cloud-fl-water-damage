A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

St. Cloud Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout St. Cloud and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A musty smell near a wall, cabinet or floor with plumbing behind it
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
