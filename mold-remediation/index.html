Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • Recent water damage or flooding that was not professionally dried

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Dry wet rugs, towels and spills right away instead of letting them sit
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Laundry rooms, baths and kitchens all need working exhaust ventilation that is sized for the space

Why Mold Remediation Is Not a DIY Job

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.
